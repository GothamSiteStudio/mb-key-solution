Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026

1. Who We Are

MB Key Solution LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website mbkeysolution.com. We are a licensed and insured (License L.C.K.0000372) mobile automotive, residential, and commercial locksmith company based in New Haven, CT.

Contact us at: Moranbodek@mbkeysolution.com or 203-258-3952.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when:

  • Booking online or requesting a quote — your name, phone number, email address, vehicle make/model/year or property location, and service details.
  • Calling, texting, or emailing us directly — your contact details and location so we can dispatch our mobile locksmith unit.

We may also collect anonymous technical data (such as browser type, device type, and pages visited) automatically when you browse our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your locksmith service inquiry or emergency lockout call.
  • Provide accurate quotes and dispatch mobile locksmith services to your location.
  • Honor our 90-Day Guarantee and maintain service records.
  • Comply with applicable state and federal laws.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies and Third-Party Services

Our website uses basic functional cookies, Google Fonts for typography, and an embedded Google Maps iframe in the footer. Google may set cookies or log requests when loading fonts or maps. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain booking submissions and customer service records for as long as necessary to provide our services, honor warranties, and comply with legal obligations. We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal information.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, please contact us at Moranbodek@mbkeysolution.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
